Mars Explorer

A Mars EVA planner for Mount Sharp, Gale Crater

The screen a crew would use inside the habitat before walking out to study the rock formations on Mount Sharp. Pick a base camp and science stops, and it finds a walkable route, checks it against life support and daylight, and draws the turn-back line: the farthest the crew can go, do the work and still get home in time.

  • 3D terrain built from NASA's 1 m elevation grid; routes avoid ground steeper than the crew's limit.
  • A solar storm drill that replays the real storm Curiosity recorded on 20 May 2024.
  • Weather, daylight and radiation for any Martian day since 2012, with estimates for future days.
  • Runs in the browser and as standalone apps for Windows, macOS and Android.

OrwellBlue

Containment and surveillance for autonomous AI agents

A system that keeps autonomous language-model agents in check: it runs them in sandboxes, verifies each action against policy, cuts them off with a killswitch when they break the rules, and writes every decision to a tamper-evident forensic ledger. I built the Next.js dashboard that operators watch it all from.

  • Scaffolded the monitoring dashboard and its typed API client.
  • A hash-chain verifier that re-checks the SHA-256 ledger, plus a full-screen lockdown when containment fires.
  • A forensic snapshot inspector with process and filesystem diffs for any agent container.
  • A one-click "rogue attack" lab and live alert toasts for critical violations.
  • Live event streaming that resumes after dropouts, with switchable live and mock data.

02 · About

Data in, decisions out

I'm Kartik, a developer who likes building things end to end: from the data and the maths behind them to the screen someone actually uses. On Mars Explorer that meant route search over a terrain grid in Python on one side, and a 3D map anyone without a space background can read on the other.

I care about interfaces that show only what's needed to decide, and about software that stays fast on a slow connection. I enjoy hackathons and team builds, where good ideas have to ship quickly.
